# 90-second demo

**0–15s:** “LeakLens helps you inspect a screenshot before sharing. Images, OCR, QR decoding, detection and redaction stay in the browser. No key or account is required.” Point to the local-processing indicator.

**15–35s:** Choose **Developer terminal**. Wait for both OCR and QR to show complete. Reveal the synthetic credential preview deliberately. Show **Credential + system context**, follow its evidence chips, and explain that neither an active key nor a compromised server is established.

**35–55s:** Choose **Travel ticket**. The passenger name is already covered in the synthetic fixture. Reveal the QR payload to show the fictional passenger details and email still encoded in it. Explain that every QR payload finding uses the whole-code image region.

**55–75s:** Select a QR child checkbox and show that the QR selections move together. Leave it selected. Turn on the manual rectangle tool, draw a region or enter coordinates, then undo/redo. Export the PNG and open the downloaded file. It contains black pixels, not removable layers.

**75–90s:** Run **Check redacted image** and explain its limits. Show **Control image**: the public URL is context, while ordinary numbers are not phone findings. Clear the session.

## Optional feature extension

After a scan, choose a sharing purpose and apply its additive suggestions, then undo a batch change. Use the **Exposure map** shortcut and **Why are these connected?** to highlight linked image evidence. Choose **Preview before downloading**, move the slider to 0% original, complete the optional review prompts, and download the reviewed PNG. Redaction edits invalidate the preview and reset confirmations.

For large screenshots with tiny text, select **Thorough** and rescan. Show the real tile progress and completed tile count. It uses more time/CPU and is not a completeness guarantee. Manual rectangles are retained, but automatic findings and selections are rebuilt.

If OCR or QR fails, show the partial-analysis warning. Do not describe it as a clean scan. All demo inputs are marked SYNTHETIC DEMO and use fictional data.
